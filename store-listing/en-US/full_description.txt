KidFocus brings learning, healthy routines, and focus time together in one family-friendly app.

🎓 AGE-APPROPRIATE LEARNING HUB

• Content for ages 2–3, ages 4–5, and grades 1, 2, and 3
• Practice math, Vietnamese, English, and thinking skills through interactive games
• Activities include counting, addition, subtraction, comparison, patterns, shapes, letters, reading, time, money, and more
• Number ranges, question counts, and difficulty adapt to the selected age or grade
• Track attempts, accuracy, and best results

✅ ROUTINES KIDS CAN FOLLOW

• Create schedules for bedtime, dinner, homework, reading, hygiene, and play
• Quickly choose workdays, weekends, weekly repeats, or individual days
• Start from suggested tasks or enter your own family activities
• Get timely reminders and mark each step complete
• Link a routine to a focus timer when needed

⏱️ A CHILD-FRIENDLY FOCUS TIMER

• Customize focus and break durations
• Use a clear interface with a friendly mascot
• Receive sound and vibration alerts when a session ends
• Track focus minutes and completed sessions each day

👨‍👩‍👧 BUILT FOR FAMILIES

• Protect parent settings with a PIN
• Create a separate profile for each child
• Choose a color theme your child enjoys
• No ads and no sale of personal data

☁️ USE OFFLINE OR SYNC

KidFocus works offline without an account. When a parent signs in with Google, schedules, settings, focus sessions, and learning progress can sync across devices.

KidFocus makes it easy to begin with one useful step: choose a learning activity, complete a routine, or focus for an age-appropriate amount of time.
